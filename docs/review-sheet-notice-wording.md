# Review Sheet: Optional Notice Wording and Email Approach

**For:** community members and advisers asked to comment.

**Background:** residents report issues to the community management by email. The standard email is short and courteous: it describes the issue, asks for a ticket number and an expected resolution date, and says the report is being tracked, with a reference to quote. The tracking is private to community residents.

The wording below is **not** in the standard email. It is held back for review and might only be offered later, for example when a report has had no response or no details. **None of it has been checked against the official legal text.**

**How to respond:** for each item in section 3, mark Keep, Change or Remove, and add a comment. Please add your name or role so we can follow up.

## 1. Optional notice wording (exact text)

> Formal notice – {community_name}, {community_location}
>
> This email is written notification of a complaint to {management_entity}, as Management Entity / managing agent of {community_name}, under Dubai Law No. (6) of 2019 Concerning Ownership of Jointly Owned Real Property in the Emirate of Dubai, and its implementing Directions.
>
> If the matter above is not addressed within 14 days of the date of this email, I will refer it to the Owners Committee of {community_name} under Article 24 of the Law. Article 24 requires the Committee to receive owner complaints on the management, operation, maintenance and repair of the Common Parts, notify them to the Management Entity, and submit them to the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA) where the Management Entity fails to address them within 14 days of being notified.
>
> I reserve my rights, including to raise the matter with the Owners Committee, with RERA / the Dubai Land Department, and before the Rental Disputes Centre. All rights are reserved.
>
> Date of notification: {date} – Deadline for response: {date + 14 days} – Unit: {unit}, {community_name}

## 2. Optional follow-up paragraph (for use after a closed ticket with no details)

> If no reply is received within 14 days I may refer the matter to the Owners Committee of {community_name}.
